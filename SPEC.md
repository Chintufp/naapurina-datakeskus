# Naapurina datakeskus — build spec

**Read this whole file before writing code.** It is the contract between everyone working on the app in parallel.
Research with sources lives in `../research/` (01 projects, 02 engineering coefficients, 03 precedents/Despro, 04 open data/tech). Use it.

## 1. What we are building and for whom

Hackathon challenge (Despro Engineering, Tampere, Oct 2026): *"Design an app or website that helps ordinary Finns understand the impacts of data-centre projects."* Pillars: (1) explore topics with short explanations, (2) try an interactive task, (3) check the sources side by side. Judged on: works as a demo; easy without a technical background; interesting and creative visualisation; **balanced and reliable information**; a clear idea that can be developed after the hackathon. Despro wants it for "Community Engagement" — open dialogue with local residents.

**Our product: "Naapurina datakeskus" (EN: "A data centre next door").** Tagline: *Sama datakeskus, eri kunta, eri tarina* / *Same data centre, different town, different story.*

The user picks their own municipality (real data for all 308 Finnish municipalities), designs a data centre with a few plain-language choices, guesses the outcomes, then plays time forward (planning → construction → 25 years of operation) and watches a living town scene and a results panel change. Every number is a **range** with a **trust label** and a **"Why?"** drawer showing the formula and sources. The same numbers live in a browsable **Sources** library. Persona "lenses" show the same results through different residents' eyes. There is **no score and no "approve the project" win condition** — the ending is the user's own priorities and the question they would ask at a residents' meeting.

Core insight to make visible: the impacts depend on the town. A 50 MW data centre's waste heat can exceed a small town's whole district-heat demand but is a fraction of a city's; 25 permanent jobs mean more in a small town with high unemployment than in Tampere.

Audience: ordinary Finns of any age with no technical background, on phones and laptops. Secondary: Despro staff showing it on a projector at a residents' evening. Primary language **Finnish**, full **English** toggle.

## 2. Non-negotiable principles (balance and reliability)

1. **Every number shown is a range** (low–high, with the typical value marked), rounded to 2 significant figures. Never a falsely precise single number for something uncertain.
2. **Every number carries a trust label**, one of four. Use the shared chips in `src/ui/components/chips.js` (`kindChip(kind)`: icon + word, never colour alone; tokens `--k-<kind>` / `--k-<kind>-soft`). Plus an evidence strength: `strong` / `medium` / `weak`.
   - `measured` (icon `ruler`, *Toteutunut* / *Reported*): a statistic, measurement, or reported figure or event about something that **already exists or has happened**. This includes an operating site's company-reported outcomes, which keep the note *Yhtiön oma ilmoitus*.
   - `estimate` (icon `doc`, *Arvio* / *Estimate*): a **forecast, plan or assessment** by a project, an authority or a consultant: EIA (YVA) figures, Fingrid forecasts, company plans and targets, proposed laws, consultant models. Not yet an outcome.
   - `calculated` (icon `calc`, *Laskettu* / *Calculated*): **this app's own calculation**.
   - `opinion` (icon `bubble`, *Näkemys* / *Viewpoint*): **a party's view, claim or interpretation**: promotional claims without a published method, political statements, residents' concerns, authorities' views.

   The rule in one line: has it happened → `measured`; is it someone's forecast or plan → `estimate`; did we calculate it → `calculated`; is it someone's view → `opinion`. FACTS use `measured`, `estimate` or `opinion`; model metrics are `calculated`. The help texts (`kind.<kind>.help` in `dict-core.js`) say the same in plain words.
3. **Every number can be traced**: a "Why?" (*Miksi?*) affordance shows the plain-language formula, every input coefficient with its value range and its source (name, type, date, link).
4. **Only calculate what can be sourced.** House prices, one data centre's effect on electricity prices, landscape/nature value, "will the town thrive" are *not* calculated — they appear as viewpoints and "nobody knows" cards.
5. **Balance ≠ 50/50.** Show benefits, drawbacks and uncertainties for every theme; label evidence strength; label company claims as company claims. Alternate/neutral ordering of pros and cons.
6. **Neutral colour language.** No red = bad / green = good. Theme hues encode identity only (energy, heat, water, land, jobs, traffic, safety, future).
7. **No score, no verdict, no win condition.**
8. **Plain language.** Finnish written for everyone (lähellä selkokieltä): short sentences, everyday words, technical terms explained on first use (glossary tooltips). Relatable comparisons: homes, football pitches, swimming pools, trucks per day, € per resident.
9. **Sources are real.** Never invent a number, quote or URL. If you cannot verify something, mark it `confidence: 'weak'` and `verified: false`, or leave it out. Use dates. Paraphrase; quote at most a few words.

## 3. Tech and conventions

- **Zero runtime dependencies.** Vanilla JavaScript ES modules, hand-written SVG/Canvas. No frameworks, no chart or map libraries, no npm install inside this folder (it is in OneDrive). Fonts from Google Fonts only (Familjen Grotesk display, Work Sans body) with system fallbacks.
- Run locally: `node scripts/serve.mjs <port>` from `app/` → `http://localhost:<port>/`. **Each agent uses its own port** (given in your task). Never use port 5173.
- Build DOM with `h()` / `s()` / `icon()` from `src/ui/dom.js`. Never inject untrusted text with innerHTML.
- **Keeping focus when you re-render**: `withFocus(root, render)` from `dom.js`. Give every control that gets rebuilt a stable `data-fk` key (`'persona-<id>'`, `'guess-lock'`, `'guess-unlock'`, `'reveal-show'`, `'lang-fi'`, `'lang-en'`, `'nav-<route>'`, `'sim-play'`, …). If focus is inside `root` on (or inside) a `[data-fk]` element, `withFocus` calls `render()` and then focuses the new element with the same key (`preventScroll`).
- **Screen-reader messages**: `announce(text)` from `dom.js` sets the text of the polite live region `#app-live` (created by `main.js`). Use it for changes that are not visible where the focus is (e.g. "Arvaukset lukittu").
- `main.js` sets `document.title` to `'<view name> – <app name>'` (home: the app name only), and after a route change (not the first load) gives the view's first `h1` `tabindex="-1"`, focuses it with `preventScroll` and announces the view name. Every view therefore starts with one `h1`.
- State: `src/store.js` (`store.get()`, `store.set(patch)`, `store.setDesign(patch)`, `store.subscribe(fn)`). Read the state shape there. store.js is scaffold-owned — don't edit it. `store.set` merges any key, so if you need extra shared state, use a new top-level key prefixed with your area (e.g. `simUi`) and keep purely local state inside your module. Cross-view hand-offs use existing keys: `townCode`, `presetId` + `design` (load a real project), `focusTheme` (topics page → simulator: highlight that theme's metrics), `persona`. `store.setDesign` clears `presetId`. Defaults: `speed: 2`; `textSize: 'normal' | 'large'` (`main.js` sets `<html data-text-size>`; `large` = root font size 112.5 %, so size text in `rem` / tokens).
- **Remembered between visits** (localStorage, validated on load, written when one of the keys changes): `'nd.lang'`, `'nd.town'`, and `'nd.state'` = JSON of `design`, `presetId`, `guesses`, `priorities`, `question`, `speed`, `textSize` (`PERSISTED_KEYS` in store.js). Nothing else is stored. Blocked storage (private mode, sandbox) only means the app starts from the defaults.
- i18n: `t(key, vars)` for UI strings from your own dictionary file; `L({fi, en})` for bilingual content objects. A UI string in a dictionary you don't own: use inline `L({ fi, en })`. Number/date formatting: `src/ui/format.js` (`fmtNum`, `fmtRange`, `fmtEur`, `fmtPct`, `fmtDate`, `fillTemplate`, `fmtMetricRange`, plus `fmtCoef`, `fmtInt`, `fmtEurRange`). Finnish formatting: `12 300`, `1,5`, `43 %`.
  - `fmtCoef(v)`: a model coefficient as stored, up to 4 decimals, no 2-significant-figure rounding (`1,15`, `0,0224`, `5 000`) — for coefficient tables and the "Miksi?" drawer.
  - `fmtInt(v)`: whole things (generators, people): `6,9 → 7`; from 100 up 2 significant figures (`1 200`).
  - `fmtEurRange(low, high)`: one unit for the range: `180–370 milj. €` / `€180–370 million`, `1,8–3,7 mrd. €`; equal ends collapse.
  - `fillTemplate(tpl, values, opts)`: placeholders `{low} {typ} {high} {value} {range}`; `values.format` `'int'` → `fmtInt`, `'eur'` → `fmtEur` / `fmtEurRange`, otherwise `fmtNum`. `{range}` and a literal `{low}–{high}` collapse to one number when both ends round to the same text ("noin 170 autoa", never "noin 170–170 autoa").
- Routing: bare hash tokens only: `#koti`, `#simulaattori`, `#aiheet`, `#lahteet`, `#suomi`, `#menetelma` (`go(view, patch)` from `src/ui/router.js`). No `#/path`, no query-string state (the app must also work when published as a claude.ai artifact, where only bare `#token` hashes survive).
- **Deep links** (also bare tokens, characters `[a-z0-9-]` only) open the simulator:
  - `#hanke-<projectId>` (e.g. `#hanke-jarvenpaa-dc`): the project's town and preset → patch `{ townCode, presetId, design (from project.preset), overrides: {}, year: 0, playing: false, simUi: {} }`.
  - `#kunta-<3-digit code>` (e.g. `#kunta-494`): → `{ townCode, presetId: null }`.
  - `#s-<code>-<mw>-<cooling>-<h1|h0>-<site>-<distanceM>` (e.g. `#s-837-150-liquid-h0-forest-500`; MW an integer 2–1000, cooling `air|evaporative|liquid`, `h1` = heat reuse on, site `industrial|forest|field`, distance 50–10000 m): → `{ townCode, presetId: null, design, overrides: {} }`.
  - `router.js` exports `projectToken(id)`, `townToken(code)`, `scenarioToken({ townCode, design })`, `shareUrl(token)` (the current address without its hash + `#token`) and `parseToken(token)` → `{ view: 'simulaattori', patch }` or `null`. On load and on `hashchange` a valid token is applied with `store.set({ ...patch, ...simEntryFields(kind, token), view: 'simulaattori' })`: `simEntryFields` (exported) gives `year: 0, playing: false, simStep: 1`, `simUi` (cleared for a project; a town or scenario link keeps the guesses and resets only `played`) and the store key `simEntry = { kind: 'project'|'town'|'scenario', token, n }` (not persisted). `views/sim.js` handles each new `simEntry` once: it stops playback, goes back to the preview year at step 1, scrolls to the town card (or the real-project card) and announces the project. The token stays in the address bar until the user changes what it describes; then the simulator replaces it with `#simulaattori`. `go('simulaattori', { presetId, … })` (the home and Finland pages' *Kokeile tätä*) and the simulator's *Lataa hanke* add the same fields and push `#hanke-<id>`. An unknown token goes home. Towns are checked against `data/towns.js`, projects against `content/projects.js`. `router.test.mjs` tests parsing and round trips.
- **Namespace-import rule** (parallel work): when your code needs a *new* export from another area's file that may not have landed yet, import that module as a namespace and fall back if the export is missing, e.g. `import * as F from '../format.js'; const fmtCoef = F.fmtCoef || ((v) => F.fmtNum(v, { sig: 3 }));`. Once both sides have landed, direct imports are fine.
- No `alert/confirm/prompt`, no `window.print`, no `<a download>` (artifact sandbox blocks them). Copy-to-clipboard via `navigator.clipboard.writeText` inside a click handler with a select-text fallback.
- localStorage only for conveniences (the keys above), always in try/catch.
- Accessibility: keyboard reachable controls, visible focus, `aria-*` on custom controls (segmented controls are `role="radiogroup"` with `role="radio"` buttons and `aria-checked`), `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (`--ink-3` is the lightest text colour allowed on `--bg`, `--surface`, `--surface-2` and `--surface-3`), touch targets ≥ 40px, no horizontal page scroll at 320–360px width, also with large text. Smallest text is `--step--2` (13 px). Links that open a new tab carry a visually hidden "(avautuu uuteen välilehteen)" (`sourceLink()` does this; `t('common.newTab')`). `base.css` has `@media (forced-colors: active)` rules so selected options, chips and focus rings stay visible in Windows contrast themes: keep selected states on `aria-checked` / `aria-pressed` / `aria-current` so they inherit them.
- Styling: only through tokens in `src/styles/tokens.css` (light + dark). Never a literal colour in component CSS; if you need a new colour, derive it with `color-mix()` from tokens. Every surface sets its own background from tokens. Both light and dark themes must look right.
- Stylesheets (linked from `index.html` in this order, and inlined in the same order by `scripts/build-single.mjs`): `tokens.css`, `base.css`, `pages.css`, `sim.css` (the stage package: everything inside `.sim-stage`), `flow.css` (the flow package: the simulator page around the stage — the *Lasketaan…* placeholders and the desktop sticky stage column), `rail.css` (simulator left rail, sim-rail), `results.css` (simulator results column, sim-results), `scene.css`. `rail.css`, `results.css` and `flow.css` contain only overrides and new classes for their own components. `build-single.mjs` inlines every `src/styles/*.css` linked from `index.html`.
- Side gutters: content wrappers use `padding-left: var(--gutter-l); padding-right: var(--gutter-r)` (already on `.page`, `.container`, header/footer inner wrappers).

## 4. Data contract (`src/data/`)

`towns.js` exports `TOWNS_META` (`{ generated: ISO date, sources: [Source] }`), `TOWNS` (array of all 308 municipalities, 2026 division) and `townByCode(code)`.

```js
{
  code: '837',                                   // Statistics Finland code, 3 digits, string
  name: { fi: 'Tampere', sv: 'Tammerfors', en: 'Tampere' },  // en = fi unless an English exonym exists
  population: 263337,                            // latest year
  households: 140000,                            // number of household-dwelling units
  employed: 120000,                              // employed labour force (työllinen työvoima)
  unemployed: 18000,                             // number of unemployed persons (derive from rate if needed)
  unemploymentPct: 13.0,
  jobs: 150000,                                  // jobs located in the municipality
  propertyTax: { generalRatePct: 1.25, revenueEur: 38900000 },   // 2026 general building rate (yleinen kiinteistöveroprosentti) and total property tax € (latest year available)
  districtHeat: { has: true, demandGWh: 2000, residentsSharePct: 80, seller: 'Tampereen Energia', estimated: false },
  //   has: town has a district-heating network; demandGWh: annual district heat used in the municipality (Energiateollisuus);
  //   if only residents-in-DH is known, estimate demandGWh = residents_in_DH × 5.9 MWh and set estimated: true.
  centroid: [x, y],                              // in the SVG coordinate system of mapPaths.js (label/dot position inside the polygon)
}
```
Use `null` for unknown values (never fake numbers). Field-level provenance goes into `TOWNS_META.sources` (name, type `statistics`, date, url) and a `TOWNS_META.notes` array explaining estimates.
A small local network that is verified but missing from Finnish Energy's statistics is entered as `districtHeat: { has: true, demandGWh: number|null, residentsSharePct: null, seller: string, estimated: true, local: true }`.

`mapPaths.js` exports `MAP = { viewBox, outline, paths: { [code]: d } }`: simplified municipality outlines projected from EPSG:3067 (ETRS-TM35FIN) to SVG units (y flipped so north is up), `outline` = whole-country outline path. Keep the file under ~350 KB; outlines must still look like Finland (the coast and Åland recognisable).

## 5. Model contract (`src/model/`)

`coefficients.js` exports `COEF`: every number the model uses, keyed (dotted keys allowed, e.g. `'pue.air'`):
```js
{ low, typ, high, unit: {fi,en}, title: {fi,en}, explain: {fi,en},      // plain-language one-liner
  confidence: 'strong'|'medium'|'weak', sources: [Source], note?: {fi,en} }
// Source = { name, type: 'authority'|'company'|'research'|'news'|'ngo'|'industry'|'politics'|'statistics'|'resident', date: 'YYYY-MM-DD' | 'YYYY-MM', url }
```

`model.js` exports:
- `DESIGN_OPTIONS` (sizes small 10 / medium 50 / large 150 / giant 500 MW kept as quick picks, plus `sizeRange = { min: 5, max: 1000 }` for a free MW choice; `design.sizeMW` is clamped to 2–1000; cooling `air|evaporative|liquid`, site `industrial|forest|field`, distance 200–3000 m).
- `THEME_KEYS = ['energy','environment','jobs','traffic','land','safety','future']`.
- `METRIC_KEYS` (by theme) — **fixed list, other areas depend on it**:
  - energy: `electricity` (GWh/yr), `gridPeak` (MW), `heatAvailable` (heat captured from the servers, GWh/yr, without heat-pump electricity), `heatPumpElectricity` (electricity the heat pumps use for the heat actually delivered, GWh/yr; 0 when heat reuse is off), `heatUsed` (heat delivered to district heating, GWh/yr, including the heat-pump electricity)
  - environment: `water` (m³/yr), `noise` (dB at nearest homes), `co2Electricity` (t/yr), `co2HeatAvoided` (t/yr), `co2Construction` (t, one-off)
  - jobs (jobs & municipal economy): `jobsConstruction` (people at peak), `jobsPermanent` (people), `jobsIndirect` (people, weak), `propertyTax` (€/yr to the municipality), `electricityTax` (€/yr to the state), `investment` (€)
  - traffic: `trucks` (heavy vehicles/day at construction peak; operation traffic as a comparison)
  - land: `land` (ha)
  - safety: `backupPower` (MW of generator fuel power)
  - future: `eWaste` (t of electronics per refresh cycle)
- `GUESS_TARGETS`: 3 guess questions `{ key, metric, comparison (comparison key or null), min, max, scale: 'log'|'linear', question: {fi,en}, unit: {fi,en} }` — keys `jobs`, `homesElectricity`, `homesHeat` (homesHeat must gracefully handle towns without district heat: question about potential homes).
- `simulate(design, town, opts) → SimResult` — pure, deterministic, fast (< 30 ms).
  - `design = { sizeMW, cooling, heatReuse, site, distanceM }`, `town` = a TOWNS entry (or null → use a documented "average Finnish town" default), `opts = { overrides: { coefKey: 'low'|'typ'|'high' }, samples = 400, seed = 1, declared }`.
  - `opts.declared` (a real project's own published figures, from `PROJECTS[].preset.declared`): `{ [metricKey]: { low?, typ, high?, upTo?: boolean, factId, note?: {fi,en}, comparisons?: { [compKey]: { low?, typ, high?, upTo?, factId, note? } } } }`. Values are in the metric's own unit: electricity GWh/yr, gridPeak MW, water m³/yr, noise dB at the nearest homes, land ha, jobsPermanent people, jobsConstruction people at peak, trucks heavy vehicles/day at the construction peak, `comparisons.operation` heavy vehicles/day in operation, backupPower MW of fuel power, eWaste t per refresh, co2Construction t, co2HeatAvoided t/yr, propertyTax €/yr, investment €, `comparisons.pctTownDH` in %.
  - Uncertainty: sample every uncertain coefficient from a triangular(low, typ, high) distribution with a seeded PRNG; report **p10 as `low`, p50 as `typ`, p90 as `high`**. An override pins that coefficient to the chosen value.
  - Town-specific: heat actually usable is capped by the town's own district-heat demand × the real-world maximum coverage seen in Finnish towns (Hamina ~70–75 %, Mäntsälä ~72 %); no network → `heatUsed` = 0 with a townContext explaining it. Jobs compared with the town's unemployed; property tax uses the town's own general rate and is compared with the town's total property-tax revenue and per resident; water compared with the town's residents' use; electricity compared with the town's households.
  - Timeline: `years` = 0..30. Phases scale with size (planning incl. zoning/EIA; construction; operation; giant sites are built in phases with capacity ramping up). Events include zoning/public hearing (OAS), EIA (YVA) for large sites, construction start, construction peak, opening, first heat delivered (if reuse), IT equipment refresh cycles, end-of-life decision around year 25–30.
```js
SimResult = {
  years: [0..30], phases: [{ key: 'planning'|'construction'|'operation', start, end }],
  events: [{ year, key, title: {fi,en}, text: {fi,en} }],
  metrics: { [metricKey]: {
    key, theme, title: {fi,en}, unit: {fi,en}, kind: 'calculated', confidence,
    series: { low: number[], typ: number[], high: number[] },     // one value per year
    headline: { low, typ, high, when: {fi,en} },                  // representative value + when it applies (WHEN.op = 'kun datakeskus on täysin käytössä' / 'once fully running')
    comparisons: [{ key, text: {fi,en}, low, typ, high, format?: 'num'|'int'|'eur', times?: true }],   // text has {low} {typ} {high} {range} placeholders (see fillTemplate)
    formula: {fi,en},                                             // plain-language formula
    inputs: [coefKey],                                            // COEF keys used
    drivers: [coefKey],                                           // the COEF keys that drive the value most
    declared: { low?, typ, high?, upTo?, factId, note?, comparisons? } | undefined,   // copy of opts.declared[key]: the project's own figure
    townContext: {fi,en} | null,                                  // Finnish starts with '<Nimi>:', e.g. "Hamina: kaukolämpöä käytetään noin 110 GWh vuodessa"
    notes: {fi,en} | null,
  } },
  cumulative: { electricity, heatUsed, heatPumpElectricity, propertyTax, co2Electricity, co2HeatAvoided },   // each { low: number[31], typ: number[31], high: number[31] }: running total through the end of year y
  meta: { …, halls: 1|2|3 (MW ≥ 300 → 3, ≥ 120 → 2, else 1), hallBuild: [{ start, end }], hallOnline: [year], heatReuseOn: boolean,
          town: { …, dhStatus: 'stats'|'statsEstimate'|'local'|'notInStats'|'default', population, households, revenue (property-tax income €/yr or null), demandGWh (may be an estimate) } },
  assumptions: [coefKey],
}
```
- Comparison keys (kept stable, other areas use them): electricity `homes` / `pctFinland` / `townHomes`; gridPeak `pctOL3`; heatAvailable and heatUsed `homes` / `pctTownDH`; heatPumpElectricity `avgMW` / `pctOfDc`; water `residents` / `pctTown`; noise `construction` / `guideNight` / `guideDay` / `guideNatureNight`; co2Electricity `net`; co2HeatAvoided `heatPumpCo2`; co2Construction `yearsOfElectricity`; jobsConstruction `carsPerDay`; jobsPermanent and jobsIndirect `pctUnemployed`; propertyTax `pctTownRevenue` / `perResident`; electricityTax `vsPropertyTax`; investment `buildings` (format `'eur'`, text uses `{range}`); trucks `operation`; land `pitches`; backupPower `generators` (format `'int'`, whole machines) / `diesel` / `testCo2`; eWaste `perYear`. `pctTownDH` is left out when `dhStatus` is `'notInStats'`; all-zero comparisons are left out; a percentage comparison with `typ ≥ 200` becomes "times" (values ÷ 100, `times: true`).

## 7. Scene contract (`src/ui/scene.js`)

`createScene(container) → { update(state), destroy() }`. Renders a living side-view (or ¾ view) of a small Finnish town into `container` (fills its width, aspect ~16:9 on desktop, ~4:3 on phones), Canvas 2D at devicePixelRatio, colours read from CSS tokens (`--sky-top`, `--ground`, `--forest`, `--house-red`, `--window-warm`, `--heat`, …) and re-read when the theme changes (`matchMedia('(prefers-color-scheme: dark)')` + `data-theme` mutation).

`state = { year, phase, phaseProgress, sizeMW, cooling, heatReuse, hasDistrictHeat, site, distanceM, townSize: 'small'|'medium'|'large', season (0..1, 0 = midwinter), trucksPerDay, workers, lang, reducedMotion, highlight: metricKey|null }`.
Optional fields (missing → today's behaviour): `halls` (1–3), `hallStates` (0..1 per hall: 0 not started, building, 1 done), `dhStatus`, `eventKey` (`'zoning'|'eia'|'permit'|'zoningDecision'|'constructionStart'|'constructionPeak'|'opening'|'expansion'|'heatFirst'|'heatWasted'|'refresh'|'endOfLife'` or null), `heatHomesShare` (0..1), `staff`, `counters` (max 3 × `{ label, value }` strings), `presentation`, `playing`, `town: { population, households }` (numbers or null), `openedAt` (fractional year the first hall opens, or null), `maxLabels` (2 on a stage under 600 px, else null), `heatAssumed` (the loaded project has not published its heat use: the heat-to-air label says *(oletus)*). The town is drawn from 4 templates by population (`town.population`, else `population`, else `townSize`): village < 5 000, small town 5 000–30 000, regional centre 30 000–150 000, city > 150 000, all at one fixed scale (the nearest homes end at the same point; the data centre is sized by log MW). Heated homes form a lit district next to the heating plant (`heatHomesShare`); a car park shows builders' cars during construction and staff cars in operation (1 car per 10 people). Under 600 px wide: at most two labels, none over the building, event labels fade after about 2 s. `hasDistrictHeat` means "draw a heating plant"; `heatReuse` means "heat flows to the plant". Counters and the phase chip sit top-left in a DOM overlay (`.scene__hud`); `presentation` scales chip, counters and labels ×1.4 and hides minor labels. With `playing === false` and no `update()` for 5 s, the animation loop stops on a still frame; the next `update()` restarts it.

Must show (from the subject's own world): Finnish red wooden houses and (for larger towns) apartment blocks, birch/spruce forest or a field or an industrial yard depending on `site`, a district-heating plant with a chimney if the town has a network, the data centre plot at a distance proportional (log) to `distanceM`. Phases: planning (survey stakes, a "kaava" sign), construction (crane, trucks on the road — count ∝ trucksPerDay, trees removed if forest), operation (hall sized by log(sizeMW), rooftop coolers with spinning fans, vapour plume for evaporative cooling, a glowing heat pipe with flowing amber dots to the town when heat reuse is on — stronger in winter, surplus shimmer in summer, power line from the grid, a generator test puff once a year). Seasons cycle within each year (snow in winter). `highlight` gently emphasises the related element (e.g. `noise` → sound rings from coolers toward the houses; `water` → plume/pipe; `trucks` → road). Small optional labels in the current language. Reduced motion: no continuous animation, static frame per state. Provide `aria-label` text summarising the scene. Performance: 60 fps on a laptop, pause the loop when the tab is hidden or the element is off-screen.

## 10. Writing style

Finnish: everyday words, active voice, short sentences, *sinä*-form, no hype, no fear-mongering. Numbers with units and an everyday comparison. Say "noin", "arviolta", "välillä … ja …" where uncertain. Company claims: "yhtiön mukaan". English: equivalent plain British English.
